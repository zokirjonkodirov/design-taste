# Notice

## Not endorsed

This project is **not endorsed, reviewed, or approved** by any designer named in it. The designers have not been asked to contribute, and none of them has agreed to the content.

## How the content was made

- The skill was written by an AI model (Claude) with a human author.
- Principles are paraphrased from public pages. Where a page was unreadable, redirected, or did not support the entry, the entry came from the author's notes and is marked **Unverified** in `skills/design-taste/references/philosophy.md`.
- No text is copied from the designers' sites. Quotes are short and attributed.

Because of this, treat each designer's entry as an interpretation, not as their words or their position.

## Trademarks and names

Designer names are used only to attribute ideas and to link to their public work. This project does not claim affiliation with any of them.

## Removal and correction requests

If you are one of the named designers, or you act for one, and you want your name removed, your entry corrected, or your link changed, open an issue at https://github.com/zokirjonkodirov/design-taste/issues. Mark the issue "removal request" or "correction". Requests are handled as follows:

- **Removal:** the entry and name are removed in the next release. The change is recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` without naming the requester beyond what they choose to share.
- **Correction:** the entry is rewritten to match your public position and marked **Checked**, or the status is lowered if you dispute it.
- **Response:** we aim to act within 14 days.

Removal does not need a reason.
