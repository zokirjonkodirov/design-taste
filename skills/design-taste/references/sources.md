The **Check** column records what was found on 2026-10-20. "Own page" means the designer's own site, bio, or news page was read; it supports a Checked status only when that page states the principle, so some "Own page" rows are Partial. "Own essay text", "Own article text", "Own book text", "Own book excerpt", and "Own book page" mean a full piece of writing by the designer was read, and those rows are Checked. "Secondary" means a publisher, review, or search excerpt was read instead, or in addition. The status itself is in `philosophy.md`.
