## Notes on the list

- Rauno Freiberg appeared twice in the source list; it is counted once here.
- The one-line summaries that seed `philosophy.md` came from the project author's notes, not from the designers themselves. They should be checked against each source before this skill is cited as the designer's own view.
- Andy Allen's approach is polarizing. The skill includes his work as a counterweight to restraint and caps it with an explicit "make it optional" rule.
